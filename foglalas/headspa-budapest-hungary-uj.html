Your scalp gets exactly what it needs!

The 4 most common scalp problems are:

  • Oily
  • Dry / Flaky
  • Hair Loss
  • Inflamed (seborrhea, psoriasis, eczema) scalp

For the first three, the Head Spa treatment can work wonders, while for more inflamed issues, it's best to first consult with a trichologist.

A MOSAIC "Hair" Head Spa treatment begins with a microscopic scalp examination, where we determine your scalp type and create a personalized hair and face mask using the right ingredients for you.

The result is radiantly beautiful hair

The effects of regular Head Spa treatments

For mild issues, one session per month may be enough. If the problems are more bothersome, it's worth doing a 3-5 session treatment course, and then maintaining with one session per month.

For oily scalp

  • Deeply cleanses the scalp, removing excess sebum and buildup.
  • Balances sebum production, reducing rapid oiliness.
  • Soothes the scalp, reducing irritation and inflammation.
  • Provides a fresh, clean feeling, keeping hair feeling refreshed for longer.
  • Does not dry out the scalp, helping prevent the return of excessive sebum production.

For dry scalp

  • Intensively hydrates and nourishes the scalp with natural ingredients.
  • Relieves itching and flakiness, eliminating the uncomfortable tight feeling.
  • Helps restore the scalp's natural protective layer.
  • Reduces dandruff and irritation caused by dryness.
  • Leaves hair softer, more flexible, and with a healthier appearance.

For hair loss

  • Stimulates blood circulation in the scalp, allowing more oxygen and nutrients to reach the hair follicles.
  • Strengthens the hair follicles, reducing excessive hair loss.
  • Deeply cleanses the scalp, removing toxins that hinder hair growth.
  • Helps activate the growth of new hair, making your hair thicker and more vibrant.
  • Its stress-relieving effect reduces one of the most common causes of hair loss.
