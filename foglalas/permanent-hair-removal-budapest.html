Does the treatment hurt?

Most of our guests feel only a pleasant coolness and a slight tingling. The Elysion Pro's built-in Crystal Freeze system cools the skin to 5 °C throughout the treatment, which numbs the surface while the laser works deep inside.

Why are several sessions needed?

The laser only reaches hairs in the growth (active) phase, and hairs are not all in this phase at the same time. That is why we repeat the treatments in line with the hair growth cycle: our programme is at most 8 sessions, but if fewer are enough, you pay for fewer.

What does permanent mean?

The laser damages the hair follicle, so treated follicles no longer produce hair: this is the “permanent” result you reach by the end of the programme. Over the years, hormonal changes can wake up dormant follicles and new hairs may appear. That is why there is the 12-month guarantee (if the hair grows back by then, we remove it free of charge), followed by maintenance treatments at half price forever.

How long does it take?

Depending on the area, one treatment takes about 30–60 minutes (full legs about 60 minutes). The first session is 60 minutes: after the assessment we start the treatment right away.

Are 8 sessions really enough?

The programme consists of at most 8 sessions, because the laser only reaches hairs in the growth phase, and these do not “mature” all at once. At the first treatment we estimate how many sessions you will need based on your skin and hair type, and you get a fixed price for it that does not change. If fewer are enough, you pay for fewer. If the hair grows back within 12 months after the 8th treatment, we remove it free of charge.

What makes the Elysion Pro different?

The Elysion Pro is a medical diode laser, one of the most powerful on the market (3,500 W), so it reaches the hair follicles more effectively than weaker machines. The cooling head protects the skin, so the treatment is comfortable and can be used safely on most skin types.

Frequently asked questions

Is the treatment suitable for all skin types?

The diode laser can be used safely on most skin types. At the first session we determine your skin type using the Fitzpatrick scale and the thickness of your hairs, and set the machine accordingly. The laser is less effective on very light, thin or grey hair: if you are unsure, come to a free consultation and we will check together.

What side effects can it have?

After the treatment, mild redness, a feeling of warmth or sensitivity may occur: this is a natural reaction and usually disappears within 48 hours. If persistent irritation, blistering or an unusual skin symptom appears, please let us know in time.

When are the results visible?

Thinning is noticeable after the very first treatment (even 20–30%). The treated hairs gradually fall out within 1–3 weeks after the treatment, and you reach the final result by the end of the 8-session programme.

Can I sunbathe during the treatments?

In the 2 weeks before the treatment avoid sunbathing and tanning beds. For 3–5 days after the treatment avoid intense heat and UV radiation (tanning bed, midday sun), and SPF 50+ sunscreen is mandatory on the treated area. If you follow this, you don't have to skip the summer season.

Does the hair really disappear “forever”?

Treated hair follicles no longer produce hair. Over the years, hormonal changes can wake up dormant follicles, so after the 12-month guarantee 1 maintenance “reminder” treatment per year may be needed, which you get at half price forever. And you can forget about everyday shaving.

What should I do with the hair between two treatments?

You can shave at any time between treatments, so your skin can stay smooth throughout. The only rule: forget removal from the root (waxing, epilator, tweezers) during the course, because it would pull out the laser's target, the hair follicle.

What happens at the first session?

The first session lasts 60 minutes and is available with a 20% discount. We determine your skin type and hair thickness, go through the contraindications (e.g. photosensitising medication, hormonal diseases), and then start the first treatment according to your skin type. Finally we estimate how many sessions you will need, and you get a fixed price for it.

How should I prepare for the treatment?

Shave the area to be treated 24 hours before your appointment so that the laser energy goes directly into the hair follicle. On the day of the treatment do not use body lotion, oil, deodorant or other cosmetics on the treated area. Do not wax or epilate in the 3–4 weeks before the treatment, because the laser needs the hair follicle. For 24–48 hours after the treatment avoid hot baths, saunas, swimming pools and intense exercise.

Is it safe in the intimate area too?

The cooling head continuously protects the skin, so the sensitive skin of the intimate areas is not damaged either, and the treatment is carried out by a professional with complete discretion.

What does the 12-month guarantee mean?

If the hair grows back within 12 months after the 8th treatment, we remove it for free. The price guarantee comes with it too: the price of the programme is fixed, you pay for only 6 of the 8 sessions, and not upfront but per session.

What happens after the 8 sessions?

After the 12-month guarantee expires, you can have the annual maintenance treatments at a 50% discount forever, that is, at half price. You won't necessarily need them, but if a few hairs reappear due to hormonal changes, we treat them quickly and at a discount.

Who is the treatment not recommended for?

We cannot treat: during pregnancy and breastfeeding; when taking photosensitising medication (e.g. some antibiotics, retinoids, antidepressants); in epilepsy; in active cancer (or within 5 years after recovery); with a pacemaker or metal implant in the area; with a tendency to keloids; in severe autoimmune disease (e.g. lupus, vitiligo in the given area).

To be postponed: after recent sunburn or sunbathing (if the skin is red or sensitive), in case of active infection in the area to be treated (e.g. herpes, fungus, open wound), and if you have waxed or epilated in the past 3–4 weeks.

We avoid and cover: tattoos and permanent makeup (we keep a safety distance), larger moles (we cover them with a white pencil).
